Path Puzzle privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Path Puzzle is a puzzle game for Android. It works fully offline and has no accounts and no servers of its own. To pay for the game it shows ads from Google AdMob when your device is online. This page explains what the app keeps on your device and what the ad service may collect.

Data kept on your device

The game saves your progress on your own phone so that you can pick up where you left off: your level, coins, booster counts, settings (sound, vibration, grid guide, theme), statistics, daily challenge completions, your best Time rush score and the level you have in progress. This data stays in the app's private storage on your device. We have no server, we never receive it, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

Ads and information collected by Google AdMob

Path Puzzle uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show ads. When your device is online, Google and its ad partners may collect and use certain information to serve and measure ads, including your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location it implies), device and operating system details, app activity such as ad impressions and clicks, and diagnostic data. We do not receive this information and do not combine it with anything else. How Google uses it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

The ads are shown in two ways: a full-screen ad now and then between levels, and optional ads you choose to watch in return for a heart or a booster. Nothing else in the game is tied to ads.

Your choices

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time in Settings under "Ad privacy choices".
  • Advertising ID: you can reset it or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads).
  • Offline: with no internet connection the game shows no ads and collects nothing.

Permissions

  • Vibration: touch feedback in the game; you can switch it off in Settings.
  • Internet and network state: used only to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service as described above.

The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or any other permission.

Children

Path Puzzle is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone. The only third party in the app is Google AdMob, which receives the information described above in order to show ads.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
